## Problem

Goals feel big until they are broken into small actions. I wanted a free, quiet canvas to map life, goals and the next steps, without an account.

## What I built

An infinite canvas you can pan and zoom. You add tasks and subjects as children of any node, rename them in place and drag them around. A details panel holds notes and subtasks.

You can link nodes across branches, with a check that stops loops. Undo and redo work with keyboard shortcuts. Pinch zoom and touch panning work on phones.

## Outcome

It is live and free to use. Your map saves on its own and stays in your browser, so there is no sign-up.

It has a light theme and a dark theme with a starfield background and glowing connectors. I tuned the motion for smooth drags and respect the reduced motion setting.
