## Hello

I am Mohammed. I study computer science in Berlin and I build AI tools. I like work that ends up in front of real people, not in a drawer.

## How I work

I start with the problem, then build the smallest version that works. I put it in front of people early and fix what they trip over. I care about privacy from day one, so I design systems to keep as little personal data as possible.

## What I am into

I enjoy computer vision, search over large piles of documents, and small tools that save people time. Away from the screen I like running and growing a community of students who want to start companies.

I want to start my own company one day. Until then I am learning by shipping.

If you want to talk, write to me. I read every message.
