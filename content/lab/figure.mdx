The figure is a cloud of points made from a photo. The photo was processed once, offline, and only the points ship to your browser: about 56,000 of them in one small binary file.

Your browser draws the points with WebGL. The sliders change how the cloud moves: how fast it turns, how many points it draws, how often it glitches, whether the scanline sweeps and how hard your pointer pushes it.

On phones it draws fewer points. Without a graphics card it shows a still image instead.
