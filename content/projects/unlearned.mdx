## Problem

I wanted to get better at explaining ideas out loud. Reading about a concept is easy. Saying it clearly with a timer running is hard.

I also wanted a steady supply of interesting ideas to learn, without picking each one myself.

## What I built

A speaking practice app. You spin a reel to land on a named concept, such as a paradox, a law or a mental model. You research it against a timer, then explain it out loud against a second timer.

It has 336 concepts across 7 domains. The app marks each one you explain and shows your progress for each domain. A quicker mode gives you a light prompt to riff on.

## Outcome

It is live and works with no account. Everything is stored in the browser, so there is nothing to sign up for.

Later I added a space themed mode with tiers of topics that unlock as you go, and a star map of your progress. The app is keyboard first, and the reel has sound effects built with Web Audio.
